# Kickoff scope and milestones

Core loop: move and dodge -> collect -> survive -> replay.

- Week 9: GDD, repository, responsive Menu/HUD/Pause and touch controls.
- Week 10: enemy AI and hazard balance.
- Week 11: local best-score and sound-preference saving.
- Week 12: audio and effects polish.
- Week 13: mobile testing and feature freeze.

One arena is the MVP. Multiplayer, accounts, shops and procedural levels are cut.

See Gatefall_GDD.pdf for the ten-part design and roles.
