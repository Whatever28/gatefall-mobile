# Pearlbound: kickoff plan

Solo project until week 14. Android, 2D side view, landscape. One 60-second dive in a ruined seabed arena. Move, kick from the seabed or a ledge, avoid sinking mines, collect three pearls and healing air bubbles, then see the rescue beacon activate at 60 seconds.

- Week 9: GDD, licensed underwater art, Menu/HUD/Pause, touch controls, safe area on three profiles, clean Git history and merged feature PR.
- Week 10: add one patrolling fish using the enemy-AI lesson and balance hazards.
- Week 11: save a local best pearl score and sound preference using the saving lesson.
- Week 12: refine audio, bubbles/explosion and damage feedback with the sound/effects lessons.
- Week 13: freeze features, test Android and all layouts, fix issues and rehearse the English video.

The week-15 MVP is one complete, stable mobile loop. No multiplayer, accounts, shop, ads, online leaderboard, inventory, procedural generation or additional worlds.
